Reading the numbers

  • Tree build: 321.0 ms for eckit vs 346.9 ms for scipy at N = 1.1M. Against the previous run (last column of the table) eckit takes 0.27× its earlier time (geometric mean).
  • With few query points (POIs) the tree build dominates at large N, and eckit takes 0.43× the original's time on average. At small N, scipy with workers=-1 takes 29.9 ms where workers=1 takes 3.2 ms and eckit 3.4 ms (2D, N = 10k, k = 1): a fixed per-call cost of workers=-1, with no counterpart in eckit's threaded search.
  • With many query points (grid to grid), eckit on 8 threads takes 1.1× the original's time on average: 1.2× in 2D and 0.97× in 3D. The slowest cases relative to scipy are 2D with small N and k ≥ 4, where scipy's multi-threaded queries are faster (1.8× at worst). Against single-threaded scipy, eckit takes 0.35× the time.
  • Since the previous run, nn_lookup makes one batch csr_search_xyz call instead of one Python call per point, eckit queries on threads (OpenMP), and its k-d tree k-nearest search is about 2× faster. Grid-to-grid cases take 0.12× their previous time, POI cases 0.59×.
  • Results agree: neighbour indices identical in at least 99.9998% of queries, distances within 1.2e-07 relative.
